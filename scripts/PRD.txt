# Overview
funnl-flow-automation is an intelligent mobile/PWA application focused on sales. Its purpose is to help sales professionals manage their customer funnel flow, daily tasks, and leverage AI to automate processes and obtain actionable information. The app aims to be attractive in design, agile in daily use, scalable, and efficient, integrating functionalities such as prospect tracking, automatic reminders, and meeting transcription/summary.

Key users: Sales representatives, sales managers, and freelancers who need to organize their sales pipeline with AI assistance (e.g., an assistant that suggests next steps or summarizes information).

Platform: Cross-platform PWA (usable on mobile as an installed app and on desktop), with Node.js/Express backend, database and authentication provided by Supabase, and integrations with external services as needed (e.g., AI APIs, external CRMs).
